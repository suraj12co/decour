How a booking runs

Four steps, in this order, every time.

  1. You send the date and the room

    A photo of the space is enough. Ceiling height and where the plug points are decide half the design.

  2. We quote within a day

    One price, itemised. If it's over your budget we'll say what to cut rather than quietly use thinner material.

  3. Half now, half on the day

    The advance holds your slot and buys the flowers. Dates inside 48 hours need the full amount up front.

  4. We set up, then disappear

    Our team leaves half an hour before your first guest, and comes back the next morning to take everything down.
